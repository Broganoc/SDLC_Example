# Project Charter

## Problem
Students request tutoring appointments by email. Staff coordinate schedules in a spreadsheet, which can delay responses and create double-bookings.

## Project Goal
Design a system that lets students search for and book available tutoring appointments.

## In Scope
- Search appointments by subject and date
- Book or cancel an appointment
- Let tutors and staff manage availability

## Out of Scope
- Video tutoring inside the system
- Payments
- Automated tutor recommendations

## Assumptions to Confirm
- Students will sign in with college accounts
- The center will define a cancellation deadline
